# Skintific Lip Gloss — 3 klip Veo (Gemini / Flow), siap tampal

**Cara guna:** Dalam Gemini (pilih Video / Veo) atau Flow → muat naik `refs/skintific_ref_label.png`
dan `refs/skintific_ref_hand.png` sebagai rujukan produk → tampal SATU prompt → jana. Ulang untuk klip 2 & 3.
Hantar 3 klip tu kepada Claude untuk cantum + sari kata + lagu + "Tekan beg kuning!".

---

## Klip 1 — Hook (≈8s)
```
Vertical 9:16, ultra realistic, handheld front-facing phone selfie at arm's length, eye level,
tiny natural hand-shake, looks like an unedited TikTok, not an ad.
CHARACTER: A Malay woman in her mid-20s, warm tan skin with real texture, soft natural makeup,
light pink bawal hijab, white oversized t-shirt.
SETTING: Passenger seat of a parked car, soft daylight from the side window, blurred greenery outside.
PRODUCT: The Skintific lip gloss from the reference images: frosted pale-pink tube, silver metallic
collar, clear pink cap, "SKINTIFIC" printed in grey on the tube. Keep its shape and label exactly.
ACTION: She gasps playfully with a hand near her mouth, then lifts the lip gloss into frame and
slowly turns it toward the lens, eyebrows rising, small laugh.
DIALOGUE (casual Malaysian Malay mixed with English, Malaysian accent):
"Guys. GUYS. Skintific tengah bagi diskaun lagi— aku dah lama pakai, brand dia memang best gila."
VOICE: Natural Gen-Z UGC delivery, medium-fast conversational pace, energetic but not shouting,
small pitch changes, rising intonation on "GUYS" and "best gila", short micro-pauses and a soft
breath between phrases, like voice-noting a close friend. Lip-sync matches every syllable.
AUDIO: Only her voice and quiet car-interior ambience. No music, no sound effects.
AVOID: subtitles, on-screen text, extra logos, robotic rhythm, presenter tone, plastic skin, extra fingers.
```
