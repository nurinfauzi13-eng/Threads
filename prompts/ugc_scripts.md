# UGC scripts & AI video prompts

## Gaya bercakap (default for every script)
Natural Gen-Z UGC delivery. Medium-fast conversational speaking pace, energetic but not shouting.
Frequent small pitch changes, slightly rising intonation on exciting words, short natural
micro-pauses between phrases. Speak like recommending a product to a close friend, not like
reading an advertisement. Playful, confident and expressive, with subtle surprise and excitement.
Use expressive eyebrows, direct eye contact and hand gestures synchronized naturally with speech.
Avoid robotic rhythm and formal presenter tone. Ultra realistic.

> AI video tools make ~8-second clips. Each scene below is one clip: paste the **Prompt**, then join
> the clips in CapCut / TikTok editor (or send them to Claude to edit + caption).
> Only use the brand's own claims. Add #affiliate / paid-partnership label when posting.

## 1. VitaHealth Asta-Glutathione + (≈30s)

**Watak (guna sama dalam setiap scene):** Malay woman in her early 20s, soft natural makeup, beige
bawal hijab, cream oversized blouse, sitting in a bright bedroom with warm daylight, filmed
handheld selfie-style on a phone, vertical 9:16.

| # | Dialog (Manglish) | Aksi |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Okay wait— korang perasan tak kulit aku lately macam... lain sikit?" | Dekat dengan kamera, kening terangkat, tunjuk pipi |
| 2 | "Ni haa, rahsia aku. VitaHealth Asta-Glutathione Plus." | Angkat botol ke kamera, senyum bangga |
| 3 | "Dia ada glutathione, astaxanthin, vitamin C, zinc— satu biji je sehari." | Kira dengan jari, buka penutup, tunjuk kapsul |
| 4 | "Dia claim tolong cerahkan kulit, kurangkan pigmentasi. And dia halal, vegan tau!" | Gerak tangan ekspresif, angguk |
| 5 | "Serious la, kalau nak try, tekan beg kuning tu. Thank me later!" | Tunjuk ke bawah, kenyit mata |

**Prompt contoh (Scene 1)** — ulang format untuk scene lain, tukar dialog & aksi:
```
Vertical 9:16 handheld selfie video, ultra realistic. A Malay woman in her early 20s with soft
natural makeup, beige bawal hijab and cream oversized blouse, in a bright bedroom with warm
daylight. She leans close to the camera, raises her eyebrows in playful surprise, touches her
cheek and says in Malay-English: "Okay wait— korang perasan tak kulit aku lately macam... lain sikit?"
Natural Gen-Z UGC delivery: medium-fast conversational pace, energetic but not shouting, small
pitch changes, rising intonation on exciting words, short micro-pauses, like talking to a close
friend. Direct eye contact, expressive eyebrows, natural hand gestures synced to speech. No
robotic rhythm, no presenter tone. No on-screen text.
```

## Rakam sendiri? Tips ikut gaya ni
- Pegang phone paras mata, cahaya dari tingkap depan muka.
- Cakap macam voice note kat kawan baik — jangan hafal ayat bulat-bulat.
- Kening naik + suara naik sikit pada perkataan "best", "plump", "diskaun".
- Rehat sekejap (½ saat) antara frasa; saya boleh potong jeda yang terlalu panjang masa edit.
- Rakam 2–3 take setiap scene, hantar semua — saya pilih yang paling natural.
